kWp (Kilowatt Peak)
The rated maximum power output of a solar system under Standard Test Conditions (STC: 1000 W/m², 25°C).
kWh (Kilowatt Hour)
Unit of energy. 1 kWh = 1 kW of power used for 1 hour. Your electricity bill is measured in kWh.
Peak Sun Hours (PSH)
The number of hours per day when solar irradiance averages 1000 W/m². Not the same as daylight hours.
Net Metering
A billing arrangement where solar users receive credit for excess electricity exported to the grid, reducing their net bill.
MPPT
Maximum Power Point Tracking. Electronic system in inverters that continuously optimizes the power harvest from solar panels.
DoD (Depth of Discharge)
The percentage of a battery's capacity that can be used. LFP batteries: 80–100% DoD. Lead-acid: 50% DoD.
String Inverter
A central inverter connected to a string (series) of solar panels. Most common and cost-effective for unshaded rooftops.
Microinverter
Individual inverter attached to each panel. Better for shaded or complex roofs. Higher cost but panel-level monitoring.
PV (Photovoltaic)
Technology that converts sunlight directly into electricity using semiconductor materials. What solar panels are made of.
STC (Standard Test Conditions)
Reference conditions used to rate solar panels: 1000 W/m² irradiance, 25°C cell temperature, AM1.5 spectrum.
ROI (Return on Investment)
Total net savings over the system lifetime divided by the net investment cost, expressed as a percentage.
LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate)
The safest and most durable lithium battery chemistry. Used in Tesla Powerwall, BYD, and Pylontech. 4000+ cycles.
